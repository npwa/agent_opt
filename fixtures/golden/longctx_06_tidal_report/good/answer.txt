Turbine T-4 failed on 14 March 2025 when its bearing seal corroded. The pilot delivered 18.7 GWh over 24 months. Cost overran the budget by EUR 4.55 million (12.4 percent). Fish monitoring found no statistically significant change in migration (p = 0.41). Lead engineer Dr. Marchetti recommended duplex stainless steel fasteners. The steering group approved Phase 2 for Q3 2026, contingent on a seal redesign.
