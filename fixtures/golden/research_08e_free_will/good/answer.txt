Libet's 1983 experiments reported a readiness potential preceding conscious intent, which some read as showing free will is an illusion. Soon et al. (2008) decoded choices from fMRI signals several seconds ahead. Schurger et al. (2012) challenged the interpretation, proposing the readiness potential reflects stochastic accumulation of noise rather than a decision. The conclusion remains contested philosophically, since compatibilists disagree that unconscious precursors undermine agency, and critics point to methodological limitations.
